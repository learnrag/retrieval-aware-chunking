# Restore Process (variant 2)

## Overview
This document describes the restore process used by service cluster 2.

## Policy
Restore requests require approval.
Related configuration keys are namespaced under `restore.v2`.

## Operations
Operators should verify the restore process after each change window.
Escalation contacts are listed in the on-call roster for cluster 2.

## Notes
Do not confuse this policy with legacy settings from cluster 1.
