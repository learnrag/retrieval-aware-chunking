# Networking (variant 1)

## Overview
This document describes the networking used by service cluster 1.

## Policy

Related configuration keys are namespaced under `network.v1`.

## Operations
Operators should verify the networking after each change window.
Escalation contacts are listed in the on-call roster for cluster 1.

## Notes
