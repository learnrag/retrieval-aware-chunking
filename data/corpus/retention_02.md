# Retention Rules (variant 2)

## Overview
This document describes the retention rules used by service cluster 2.

## Policy
Backup retention is 30 days.
Related configuration keys are namespaced under `retention.v2`.

## Operations
Operators should verify the retention rules after each change window.
Escalation contacts are listed in the on-call roster for cluster 2.

## Notes
Do not confuse this policy with legacy settings from cluster 1.
